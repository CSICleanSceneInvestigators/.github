## After the Tape Comes Down

CSI: Clean Scene Investigators provides discreet, trauma-informed cleanup for the scenes families, property owners, businesses, and communities should never have to face alone.

Founded by a former Crime Scene Investigator, CSI combines forensic awareness, operational precision, and compassionate communication throughout the restoration process.

## Specialty Cleanup Services

- Crime scene and trauma cleanup
- Biohazard remediation
- Unattended death and decomposition cleanup
- Hoarding cleanup
- Forensic odor removal
- Vehicle biohazard cleanup
- Extreme and sensitive cleanup situations

CSI serves Dallas-Fort Worth, Denton County, and communities across North Texas with 24/7/365 mobile response.
